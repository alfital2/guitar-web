Safari can't choose the output device. It ignores the dropdown above and always uses your Mac's system default output. For this to reach your Focusrite (and stay low-latency), set System Settings → Sound → Output → Scarlett Solo (and Input → Scarlett Solo). Otherwise audio goes to your Mac speakers with extra delay.
