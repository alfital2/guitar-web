## Per-effect DSP + probe numbers

### 1. `acousticsim` — Acoustic Sim (body, air, level)
Feedforward EQ chain: gentle HP @90Hz → peaking 105Hz (air cavity, 0..+9dB) →
peaking 230Hz (top plate, 0..+7dB) → high-shelf 6.5kHz "air" (0..+12dB) →
level (default 5 → unity).
- **Probe (body-resonance signature):** wet/dry ratio per harmonic on the
  sawtooth burst — `boost220=1.743` (on the 230Hz body peak) vs `boost660=1.048`
  (outside both resonances); sustained-tone `spectralDist=0.3066` vs dry.

### 2. `distortion` — Distortion (dist, tone, level)
DS-1-style: 250Hz tightening highpass → new `makeDistortionCurve` in
`src/dsp.js` (steep linear gain `k = 1 + dist*15` slammed into hard ±1 rails,
origin-true, 4x oversample) → lowpass tone (900–7000Hz) → level. Deliberately
distinct from `drive` (tanh soft clip) and `fuzz` (saturating asymmetric knee).
- **Probe (harmonic growth):** `wetHarm=0.1081` vs `dryHarm=0.0006`
  (2f/3f/4f on the 220Hz sustain); silent-gap floor `0.00000` (origin-true).

### 3. `harmonizer` — Harmonizer (interval −12..+12st default +7, mix, level)
Dry passes untouched at unity; a parallel `pitchshift-processor`
AudioWorklet voice (its internal mix pinned to 1.0 = fully wet) is blended in
by the Mix knob; level default unity.
- **Probe (shifted-peak signature):** harmony voice at +7st ⇒ 220Hz → 329.6Hz;
  Goertzel on sustain: `wetMag=0.0205` vs `dryMag=0.0009` (>4x dry + absolute
  floor). The crossfade shifter spreads some energy into sidebands, hence the
  peak is smaller than an ideal sinusoid's — still 22x the dry magnitude.

### 4. `univibe` — Uni-Vibe (speed 0.1–8Hz default 3, intensity, mix)
Four all-pass biquads at **staggered** centers (350/580/1100/2200Hz — the
original's unequal caps) all swept by **one** shared **unipolar** LFO
(osc×0.5 + 0.5 constant → [0,1]; each stage adds `depth×center` above a
positive base of `center×0.5`, so the sweep can never go negative — same
clamping-bug class the 2026-07-01 flanger fix addressed, guarded by a unit
test). The lamp also throbs the wet level (photocell AM, up to 25% at max
intensity). Mix 0.5 = chorus mode, 1.0 = vibe/vibrato mode. Returns
`destroy()` stopping both started sources.
- **Probe (modulation + throb):** sustained-tone `spectralDist=0.2133` vs dry;
  envelope throb `envDepth=0.658`, `measuredHz=2.94` vs speed=3Hz.

### 5. `springverb` — Spring Reverb (tension, decay, mix)
ConvolverNode + new `makeSpringImpulse` in `src/dsp.js`: a **dispersive**
drip train — every `spacing` seconds (tension 0..10 → 60..30ms) a short
windowed **downward chirp** (~4.2kHz → ~500Hz over ≤25ms, the spring "boing")
over a decaying darkened-noise wash, with per-channel jitter for stereo;
12ms pre-delay; decay 0..10 → 0.6..3.5s IR. Impulse rebuilds only when
tension/decay move (reverb.js guard pattern, unit-tested).
- **Probe (inter-burst tail):** `wetGapRms=0.00534` in the silent gap vs
  `dryGapRms=0.00000` (threshold max(3×dry, 0.002)).

### 6. `whammy` — Whammy (bend 0..10 default 10, range 1..24st default 12, mix)
`pitchshift-processor` voice whose semitone target = `bend/10 × range`,
**smoothed** with `setTargetAtTime` (30ms glide) on every knob move after the
initial set — riding Bend gives the continuous dive/soar. Default = classic
+1-octave full-toe scream at mix 1.
- **Probe (bend signatures, two renders):** bend=10 ⇒ +12st peak @440Hz:
  `wetMag=0.0275` vs `dryMag=0.0000`; re-render with bend=0 is near-dry:
  `zeroMag=0.0002` (0.7% of the bent peak) and `specZero=0.0062` vs
  `specBent=0.4464` (spectrum collapses toward dry).

## The probe-found bug fix (octave DC hum)

`makeRectifierCurve` in `src/dsp.js` computed `depth*(|x|*2−1) + (1−depth)*x`,
which is `−depth` at `x=0` — the octave pedal emitted a constant **~0.279 RMS
DC offset during silence** (hum whenever the guitarist wasn't playing).
Fixed to `depth*|x| + (1−depth)*x`: `f(0)=0`, endpoints `f(±1)` unchanged,
rectified 2f (octave-up) content preserved.
- **Verified by probe:** octave silent-gap floor **0.27887 → 0.00009 RMS**
  (now *below* drive `0.00146`/fuzz `0.00548` whole-gap figures; on the
  matched 50ms-in window: drive 0.00000, fuzz 0.00020, octave 0.00009), and
  the octave harmonic-growth signature still passes (probe row PASS,
  `spectralDist=0.1157`).
- The harmonic-family "silent input stays silent" check was **promoted from
  informational to gating**, and now measures from 50ms into the gap so a
  high-gain pedal's legitimate few-ms filter ring-out of the burst's abrupt
  edge (distortion's ~91x gain amplifies it to a brief clipped click) isn't
  mistaken for DC leak — a constant origin-offset still dominates the
  remaining 350ms window.
- **Focused unit tests:** `tests/dsp.test.js` — origin pass-through for all
  depths (WaveShaper center-sample midpoint), even symmetry at depth 1,
  endpoint preservation, identity blend at depth 0.

## Notes / caveats

- `univibe`'s throb-rate estimate uses the existing mean-crossing method; the
  sustain window (~0.85s) holds ~2.5 cycles at the 3Hz default, so the check
  accepts a generous 0.35x–3.5x band around the speed param (measured 2.94Hz,
  ~2% off — comfortably inside).
- `harmonizer`/`whammy` reuse the existing `pitchshift-processor` worklet;
  like `pitchshift`, they are k-rate per render quantum and share its known
  crossfade-shifter artifacts on large shifts.
- The probe report table in `.superpowers-probe-report.md` was intentionally
  NOT regenerated (its narrative documents the pre-fix octave finding); this
  file carries the post-change numbers.
